Privacy policy

Mawaqeet (app.nileorbit.mawaqeet) by NileOrbit · Last updated 12 November 2026

In short: Mawaqeet collects no data about you. It needs no account, has no ads, analytics or trackers, and everything it saves stays on your phone. Its only internet connection fetches the weather for the clock widgets, using only an approximate location for your city.

What the app keeps on your phone

  • Your location: the city you choose, or your approximate location if you allow it, used only to calculate prayer times.
  • Prayer log: the prayers you mark as prayed.
  • Your settings: calculation method, alerts, sounds, appearance and widgets.
  • Custom adhan: if you pick an audio file from your phone, the app plays it from where it is and never sends it anywhere.

None of this leaves your phone (except the rounded location used to fetch the weather, below), and neither we nor anyone else can see it.

Location

The app asks only for approximate location, reads it only while the app is open, and never in the background. You can decline and choose your city by search or coordinates instead; every feature still works.

Weather on the clock widgets

The Big Clock, Gold Clock and Clock & Prayer widgets show the temperature, humidity and chance of rain. To get them, the app contacts the free Open-Meteo service about once an hour and sends it your city's coordinates rounded to a tenth of a degree (about 10 km), so it never receives your exact place. Nothing else is sent: no name, account, device identifier or prayer log. As with any internet connection, the service sees the connection's IP address; see Open-Meteo's terms and privacy policy. This happens only while a clock widget is on your home screen, and with no internet the weather simply disappears and the widget works as before.

Backup and moving to a new phone

The prayer log and settings (including your location) are excluded from Android's cloud backup. They move only in a direct transfer from your old phone to your new one (by cable or a direct connection), which happens between your two devices and never passes through us.

Backup file

From Settings → Backup you can export one file holding all your settings (including your location and Calculation Method), the prayer log and the adhkar record. The file is made only when you ask for it and is saved where you choose with your phone's file picker (the phone's files, Google Drive, a message to yourself…). The app never sends it anywhere and it never passes through us; it needs no internet and no permission. The file is not encrypted and holds your location and worship record, so keep it somewhere private.

Permissions

  • Notifications: for adhan alerts and reminders.
  • Alarms & reminders (exact alarms): so the adhan sounds on the exact minute.
  • Full-screen notifications: to show the adhan over the lock screen in alarm-clock mode, if you turn it on.
  • Foreground media playback: so the adhan plays to the end, with a notification that has a Stop button.
  • Run at startup: to reschedule alerts after the phone restarts.
  • Internet: only to fetch the weather for the clock widgets, as above.
  • Vibration and approximate location, as above.

Deleting your data

Settings → "About & your data" → "Delete Prayer Log". Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data.

Children

The app collects no data from any user, children included.

Changes to this policy

If anything changes in how the app handles data, we'll update this page and its date before releasing the version with that change.

Contact
